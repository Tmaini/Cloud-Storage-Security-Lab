What this review cannot see

Account and bucket Block Public Access settings, ACLs, other IAM policies, encryption configuration, and actual access logs. Conditions and deny statements need contextual review. A policy finding does not prove that a bucket is public.

02 / UNDERSTAND

Three layers to think about.

01

Who can access it?

A principal identifies who a policy applies to. An Allow with a wildcard principal deserves review because it can include anyone. Conditions and other controls still matter.

Read about Block Public Access ↗
02

What can they do?

Least privilege means granting only the actions and resources needed. A reader typically needs a narrower set of permissions than s3:*, which covers all S3 actions.

Explore bucket policy examples ↗
03

How does data travel?

A deny rule using aws:SecureTransport can restrict non-HTTPS requests. Encryption in transit is separate from encryption at rest, which this lab does not inspect.

Learn about condition keys ↗
